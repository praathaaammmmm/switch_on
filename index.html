Help

Questions people ask first

Still stuck?Message us from your booking confirmation and a person will reply, 8 AM to 10 PM every day.
How accurate is the base estimate?

It is the starting price for the most common models. The shop confirms the final quote after examining your device, and nothing happens until you approve it.

Who actually repairs my device?

A verified repair shop near you. You’ll see the shop’s name and rating as soon as your device is assigned.

Is my device safe during pickup?

The pickup partner confirms a one-time code with you, photographs the device and seals it in a tamper-evident bag at your door.

How long will I be without my device?

Most phone repairs come back the same day. Water damage and laptop screen repairs can take one to four days. The estimator shows the time for your repair.

What if I don’t accept the final quote?

We bring your device back, free of charge.

Do I need to be home for the pickup?

Someone needs to hand over the device and share the pickup code. It can be a family member or a neighbour.
